How to find your Phone's IP Address
🍏 On iPhone (iOS):

1. Open Settings → tap Wi-Fi.
2. Tap the blue ⓘ info icon next to Lapin Wifi.
3. Scroll down to see your IP Address (e.g. 192.168.1.56).

🤖 On Android:

1. Open Settings → tap Network & internet / Wi-Fi.
2. Tap the ⚙️ Gear icon next to Lapin Wifi.
3. Tap Advanced or View more → see your IP address.
